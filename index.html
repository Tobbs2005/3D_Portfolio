Skateboarding Coach

⇠ scroll ⇢

Support Vector Machines K-Nearest Neighbours Matlab

In 11th grade, I represented Hong Kong at the International Science and Engineering Fair (ISEF). I traveled to Dallas, Texas, where I presented my project, “Detection of Skateboarding Tricks Based on Gyroscopic Data.” After using a gyroscope and accelerometer to collect data samples of skateboarding tricks being performed, I 3D-printed a small box to hold an ESP32 processor and an MPU9250 gyroscope & accelerometer.

With that, I gathered more than 100 samples of various skateboarding tricks, feeding them into my model, which was trained using algorithms like Support Vector Machines and K-nearest neighbors. The device would detect the tricks and classify them.

Additionally, by collecting data samples of commonly made mistakes, the device can detect mistakes a skateboarder makes during their performance and provides pre-written user-specific feedback through a developed application on what to do to improve.

I was able to take this project a step further, composing a 35-page research paper titled: "Investigating the Accuracy and Prediction Speed of K-Nearest Neighbours and Support Vector Machines on the Classification of Skateboarding Tricks Using Inertial Measurement Unit Signals".

CollegePilot

⇠ scroll ⇢

TypeScript React Supabase Edge Functions Postgres/pgvector RAG LLMs

CollegePilot is an AI college-counseling platform I built and independently scaled to 5K+ daily active users. It guides high-school students through the whole application journey: personalized school matching with reach/target labels, essay drafting and scoring, an activities finder, deadline tracking, and an AI counselor that checks in on your progress.

Under the hood, a RAG layer fetches only the relevant pieces of a student's essays, activities, and the colleges database — Postgres/pgvector with 384-dim embeddings and cosine search — instead of pasting everything into the prompt, giving sharper, grounded feedback at far lower token cost. I also ran A/B tests on the UI/UX, onboarding, and funnels to improve activation and retention.

StonksGPT

NextJS TypeScript Alpaca API MCP ElevenLabs LLMs

StonksGPT is a full-stack AI trading assistant that combines real-time market data, voice-powered conversations, interactive charting, and safe trade execution through the Alpaca Trading API and the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

Users can manage their portfolio, analyze stocks with side-by-side comparison charts, stay current with market news, and place orders — all through a dashboard with natural-language chat and ElevenLabs-powered voice calls. The assistant supports configurable LLM backends (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Dedalus Labs), generates charts automatically while you chat, and keeps trading safe with position-aware order management.
